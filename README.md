# 🔧 Mechanical Menaces — FLL Challenge Team #76222

Welcome to the GitHub repository for the **Mechanical Menaces**, a FIRST LEGO League (FLL) Challenge robotics team from Wisconsin!

We are a rookie Challenge team of six students, mostly third graders (ages 8–9). We love building LEGO robots, solving problems, trying new ideas, and having fun along the way!

## 🤖 What's in This Repository?

We wanted to share some of the things we designed and learned during our FLL season. Feel free to explore our work and use our ideas as inspiration for your own team!

### 1. [Robot Design](photo/) & [Build Instructions](fll26.io)

We started with a basic robot design called Coop Bot, but quickly decided we wanted to build our own robot.

After several rounds of building, testing, and improving, we created **[Version 3](photo/IMG_1110%20Large.jpeg)**, our final competition robot.

Our design focuses on:

- A strong, sturdy frame
- Reliable driving and turning
- Easy-to-change attachments
- A design that works for multiple missions

We've included **[build instructions](fll26.io)** so other teams can see how we put our robot together.

Download and open the [robot design file (.io)](fll26.io) with **[BrickLink Studio (Stud.io)](https://www.bricklink.com/v3/studio/download.page)** to explore the model and its building steps.

### 2. [Pybricks Programming Code](Pybricks%20code/)

We started programming with LEGO SPIKE Prime Blocks, which we already knew from LEGO League Explore.

Then our mentor introduced us to **[Pybricks](https://pybricks.com/)**!

We found that Pybricks made it easier to write programs, make changes, and improve the accuracy of our robot's movements.

We've shared our **[Pybricks code](Pybricks%20code/)** so other teams can see how we programmed our robot to complete missions.

### 3. [Attachment Designs & Pictures](photo/)

We designed and built different attachments to help our robot complete missions.

One of our favorite design ideas was creating a **[common attachment frame](photo/IMG_1420%20Large.jpeg)**. Our attachments drop onto the robot using gravity and long LEGO pins, making them easy to switch without complicated connections.

Our attachments include ideas using:

- Rubber grips to move objects
- [LEGO nets to catch or carry pieces](photo/IMG_1415%20Large.jpeg)
- Rubber bands and forks for catching and positioning
- [LEGO tread links to create a conveyor belt](photo/IMG_1418%20Large.jpeg)

We've included **[pictures of our attachments](photo/)** so other teams can see our designs and maybe get ideas for their own!

## 💡 Why Are We Sharing Our Work?

One of the things we've learned in FIRST LEGO League is that **sharing ideas helps everyone learn**.

We hope our [robot design](photo/), [attachments](photo/), and [code](Pybricks%20code/) can help other teams try something new, solve a problem, or come up with an even better idea.

You don't have to build things exactly the way we did. Experiment, make changes, and find what works for your team!

## 🌟 FIRST Core Values

As a team, we try to practice the six FIRST Core Values:

**Discovery • Innovation • Impact • Inclusion • Teamwork • Fun**

We learned that things don't always work the first time, and that's okay! We kept testing, changing our designs, helping each other, and learning along the way.
